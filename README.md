# Change Point Analysis and Statistical Modeling of Brent Oil Prices

## Project Overview

This project analyzes historical Brent crude oil prices to identify structural changes caused by major geopolitical, economic, and policy events. Using Bayesian Change Point Detection, the analysis quantifies significant shifts in oil price behavior and associates them with real-world events such as OPEC production decisions, wars, economic sanctions, and global financial crises.

The project is developed as part of the **10 Academy Artificial Intelligence Mastery Program - Week 10 Challenge**.

---

# Business Problem

Brent crude oil prices are highly sensitive to geopolitical events, economic shocks, and policy decisions. Investors, policymakers, and energy companies require timely insights into how these events influence market behavior to improve investment strategies, risk management, and operational planning.

This project aims to detect statistically significant structural breaks in historical Brent oil prices and relate them to major global events.

---

# Project Objectives

The primary objectives are to:

- Analyze historical Brent oil price movements
- Investigate long-term trends and volatility
- Perform stationarity analysis
- Apply Bayesian Change Point Detection using PyMC
- Quantify the impact of significant market events
- Develop an interactive dashboard for result visualization
- Communicate findings through professional reports and visualizations
